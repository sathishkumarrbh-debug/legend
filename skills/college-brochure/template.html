About the Programme

The FDP has been designed to provide faculty members, researchers and students with updated knowledge of how Artificial Intelligence and digital technologies are transforming Mechanical Engineering. Experts from reputed academic institutions will deliver lectures and interact with the participants.

Programme Objectives

  • To introduce the fundamentals of AI and digital transformation in Mechanical Engineering.
  • To expose participants to AI in robotics, automation, Industry 4.0 and smart factories.
  • To explore digital technologies in additive manufacturing and machine learning applications.
