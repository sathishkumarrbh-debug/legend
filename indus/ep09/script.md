## Description
4,500 years ago, almost every home in Mohenjo-daro had its own bathing area: watertight brick floors sloping to a drain. At Harappa, archaeologists found what look like toilets in almost every house: a big jar sunk in the floor, and beside it a small pot of water, like a lota. Dirty water ran into covered brick street drains, some big enough for a worker to climb in and clean, and the city may have had over 700 wells. In 1858, London's river stank so badly that Parliament ordered new sewers. Mohenjo-daro had covered drains more than 4,000 years earlier. No palace we can find. Their best engineering went into ordinary homes.
