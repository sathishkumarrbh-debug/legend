# Indus Files: series plan (Jambudvipa Files)

One Short every 2 days (about 3 weeks), then a long-form documentary built from all parts.
Each episode answers its own hook, then ends on the next episode's question (fx.ending + pinned comment teaser).

Unicorn wording rule: say "no other civilisation of its time used this symbol" / "only the Indus people made it". NOT "found nowhere else": real unicorn seals were carried to Mesopotamia by trade.
Unicorn photos: real seals only (Met Museum open access CC0 "unicorn and incense burner" seal; Wikimedia Commons museum photos, licence-checked), plus a real one-horned terracotta figurine if a free photo exists.

Optional: merge 9 and 10 (Mohenjo-daro + Lothal) to make 11 parts.

## Hype system (every episode)
1. Ending animation: next question + "INDUS FILES #N".
2. Pinned comment: next-part teaser + "Comment PART N"; reply to own pin with a yes/no question.
3. "Indus Files" playlist in every description; Shorts "Related video" = previous part.
4. Community poll the day before each release.
5. Pin the latest part on the channel page; from Part 6 say "Part 11 = finale".
6. Same identity: gold INDUS FILES type, dark question ending, Yash voice.

## Care rules
- No India vs Pakistan framing; no political/communal angles (Aryan debate only as "largely rejected by archaeologists" if at all).
- Pashupati: "some scholars see…, others argue…"; Part 4: "no clear evidence of kings" (interpretation, not proof).
- Real photos for real objects (Wikimedia, licence-checked); AI images labelled "Artist's impression".
