**Title:** This 1,600-Year-Old Iron Pillar Refuses to Rust 🇮🇳

**Description:**
Sixteen centuries in the open, and the Iron Pillar of Delhi has barely rusted. Here's the secret ancient Indian ironsmiths built into the iron 👇

Made around 400 CE in the time of the Gupta emperor Chandragupta II, the pillar was forge-welded by hand from pieces of hot iron, over six tonnes in all. Historians believe it first stood at Udayagiri in Madhya Pradesh. Its secret is phosphorus-rich iron that slowly grew a thin protective layer, about as thick as a human hair.
