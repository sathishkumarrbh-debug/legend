## Description
In 1780, Kuyili, commander of Rani Velu Nachiyar's women soldiers, is remembered for walking into the enemy's weapons store at Sivaganga and destroying it. Most Indians have never heard her name.

Velu Nachiyar lost her husband, the King of Sivaganga, in 1772. With help from Hyder Ali and the Maruthu brothers she built an army and took her kingdom back in 1780. The story of Kuyili's final attack comes mainly from later accounts and oral tradition, so treat the details as tradition, not recorded fact.
