## Description
Chittor, 1536. Banvir had seized Mewar and wanted the young heir, Udai Singh, dead. According to Rajput tradition, the prince's nurse, Panna Dai, smuggled Udai out of the fort in a fruit basket and laid her own son in the prince's bed. Her son was killed in his place.

Panna took Udai to Kumbhalgarh, where he was raised in hiding. In 1540 Udai Singh II won back Mewar, and he later founded Udaipur, the City of Lakes.

Banvir's usurpation and Udai Singh's restoration are history. The details of Panna's sacrifice come from Rajput tradition, recorded by James Tod in 1829, so treat them as tradition rather than eyewitness record. Udai Singh was born in 1522, so he was a boy of about 14 at the time, not a baby. The name "Chandan" often given to Panna's son appears only in later retellings.
