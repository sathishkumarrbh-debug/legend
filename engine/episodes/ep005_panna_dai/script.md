# EP5 Panna Dai: final script (Yash)
On-screen hook from 0.0 s: SHE SACRIFICED HER SON

1. She saved a prince... by giving up her own son.
2. Chittor, 1536. Banvir has seized Mewar and killed its ruler.
3. Now he wants the young heir, Udai Singh, dead.
4. Tradition says the prince's nurse, Panna Dai, heard the cries from the palace.
5. She hid Udai in a fruit basket, and a trusted servant carried him out of the fort.
6. But Panna stayed behind.
7. She laid her own son in the prince's bed.
8. Banvir burst in, sword drawn, and demanded the prince. Panna could not speak... she pointed to the bed.
9. Her son died in the prince's place.
10. Panna reached Kumbhalgarh with Udai, and he was hidden there for years.
11. In 1540, Udai Singh took back Mewar. Years later, he founded Udaipur, the City of Lakes.
12. A kingdom lived on... because one mother chose duty over her own child.

Fact notes: 1536 commonly given (some say 1537). Udai Singh born 1522 per modern sources (about 14); Tod (1829) says about six, so no "baby". Bed/basket/pointing scene = tradition (Tod), labelled on screen. Son named Chandan in later retellings, not in Tod, so not named. Udaipur founding date disputed (1553/1559), so no year. 1540 recovery of Mewar widely accepted.
