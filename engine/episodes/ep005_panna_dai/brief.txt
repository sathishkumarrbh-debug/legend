KEY FACTS I have (please verify, and tell me which parts are tradition, e.g. from James Tod's Annals of Rajasthan, 1829):
- 1536: Banvir, a relative who had seized power, murdered Vikramaditya, the ruler of Mewar, then went to kill the young Udai Singh, the last heir (son of Rana Sanga).
- Udai Singh's nurse Panna Dai had the prince carried out of the fort in a basket by a servant (named Kirat/Keerat in some accounts).
- She put her own son, Chandan, in the prince's bed. Banvir killed Chandan, believing he was the prince.
- Panna took Udai Singh to Kumbhalgarh, where the governor Asha Shah Depura protected him.
- 1540: Udai Singh took back the throne. He later founded Udaipur (around 1559).
